why bother

No two hands are alike.

Fonts are usually made by experts, for everyone. This one is made by you, for you. It carries the small, lovely things about the way you write.

Personality

The way you cross a t or loop a g says something about you before a single word is read. A font made from your hand keeps all of it.

Uniqueness

There are billions of people, and not one of them writes an a the way you do. That is a small miracle, and it deserves to be kept.

The handwritten word

Typed words are fast. Handwritten words are warm. Now a birthday card, a recipe or a note on the fridge can be both.

how it works

Four gentle steps.

No design tools, no font editor, no tracing by hand. Just a pen, your phone and an agent that knows what to do.

Print

Download the Fountain sheet and print it at full size. Any home printer and any plain paper will do.

Write

Fill each box with a pen you like. Take your time, and write the way you actually write. Wobbles are welcome.

Snap

Photograph both sheets flat on a table in daylight, with the four corner marks in view. Your phone is plenty.

Hand it over

Give the photos and the Fountain skill to your AI agent. It traces, tidies, spaces and kerns your letters, then hands you a font file.

the goods

The Fountain sheet

Two printable pages with a box for every letter, number and mark, plus a few ruled lines for a sentence you love. Print at full size, no scaling.

US Letter PDF A4 PDF Open in your browser

The skill

A skill file that teaches your AI agent the whole craft: finding each letter in the photo, tracing it, straightening the sheet's little distortions, then spacing, kerning and compiling a real TTF.

Download the skill

Works with Claude Code and any agent that reads skills. Unzip it into your skills folder, then just ask:

unzip handwriting-to-font.skill -d ~/.claude/skills/
claude "Here are photos of my Fountain sheets. Make me a font."

a note, in closing

Keep your hand.

Somewhere there is a shoebox with a letter in it, and you know who wrote it before you read a word. That is what handwriting does. It is a face for words.

We built Fountain so that face can come along to the places we type now: a message, a card, a recipe, a website like this one. Nothing about your writing needs fixing. It only needs keeping.

So print the sheet, make a cup of something warm, and write your letters the way you always have. The rest is just water finding its way.
